# IKS Atlas

An easy way to find Indian Knowledge Systems references for Classes 9 and 10.

## Download Android

[Download IKS Atlas 0.2.0 APK](https://github.com/aishKodes/IKS_AI-Releases/releases/download/v0.2.0/IKS-Atlas-Android-v0.2.0-public.apk) · [Release notes and checksum](https://github.com/aishKodes/IKS_AI-Releases/releases/tag/v0.2.0)

Install the APK, open the app and choose a class, subject, topic and subtopic. Tap **Find connections** to see matching references. You can also search directly. No account, data download, source-file import or AI key is needed.

The complete offline collection includes **136 research mappings** covering Biology, Chemistry, Economics, Mathematics and Physics across Classes 9 and 10. The corpus owner has confirmed review and authorized public distribution. Exact reference text is retained; external link availability has not been checked. A historical connection does not imply scientific equivalence.

## What changed in 0.2

A simple selector-first layout, readable Manrope typography, colourful subject accents and clear reference cards. The primary navigation is **Find / Saved / More**. Extra research and teaching tools are available when needed. Bookmarks, notes and teaching material remain local and survive this update.

The universal APK uses the same persistent signing key as the previous internal pilot. Verify the download against the attached SHA256 file. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser or file manager. The app needs no storage or contact permission for ordinary browsing.

## Availability and rights

This repository provides publicly downloadable app releases. Source development remains in a separate private repository; no original research documents or private credentials are included here. Dataset ownership remains with its research rights holders; permission to download the app does not grant unrestricted republication rights. Installed structured records can be extracted.

Windows installers are not available yet. Optional AI needs a user-supplied provider key and explicit sharing authorization; it is not required for lookup or offline teaching templates. No public editorial server is required.
